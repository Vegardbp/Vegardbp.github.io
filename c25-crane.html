C25 Crane

Raspberry Pi 5 · Pico 2 (C++/Arduino) · Python · Fusion 360 · Blender · KiCad

A 1:25 scale, fully functional replica of Seaonics' C25 offshore crane, built as a portable demonstration rig that plugs directly into Seaonics' real production control stack and HMI.

Bachelor thesis in Automation Engineering (NTNU), completed with a group of four for client Seaonics AS.

My primary contributions were the python-based interface between seaonics control system, and physical motor control nodes. As well as most of the 3D design of the crane, 3D printing, and cable routing.

The crane reproduces slew, luffing, telescope, and winch motion driven live from Seaonics' industrial control system, translating real operator joystick input into scaled physical motion through a deterministic, bare-metal motor control layer.

Demo

Features

4-axis motion Independently driven slew, luffing, telescope, and winch axes, each on a dedicated stepper motor and gearbox
Live Seaonics integration Runs against the real Seaonics production control stack and HMI over TCP/IP, not a simulated stand-in
Distributed real-time control Raspberry Pi 5 master handles industrial comms and kinematics; four bare-metal Pico 2 edge nodes generate deterministic microsecond STEP pulses
Custom motor driver PCBs Two hand-designed PCBs hosting MP6500 stepper drivers, with shared ground plane and per-driver bulk decoupling
Industrial-style power distribution Four isolated PSUs, separated MCB protection groups, fused DC branches, and star-topology PE grounding
Hardware auto-discovery Custom UART handshake protocol that maps each Pico controller to its COM port automatically at boot
Relay-based e-stop Hardware emergency stop and limit switches that preserve motor holding torque instead of just cutting power
Thermal-aware motor tuning Driver current settings tuned against measured thermal imaging data to keep motors within safe operating limits
Joystick operator interface Thrustmaster T.Flight Stick X mapped directly to crane axes through the Seaonics control system
Travel-ready cabinet Fully self-contained control cabinet with HMI, joystick, and power system built for conference demos
